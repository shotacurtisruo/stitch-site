Quick answers

How do I get a good stitch? Take screenshots that overlap — scroll a little less than one full screen between captures so each screenshot shares some content with the previous one. Select them in top-to-bottom order.

A seam looks off. Tap Adjust on the result screen, pick the seam, and slide until the transition disappears. Low-confidence seams are flagged automatically.

Why don't I see a photo in the picker? Stitch shows your Screenshots album. Images received through chat apps aren't tagged as screenshots by iOS, so they won't appear there.

Restore a purchase: Settings → Restore Purchase (same Apple Account).
