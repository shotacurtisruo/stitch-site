Privacy Policy

Your screenshots never leave your device.

  • All stitching happens locally on your iPhone. Stitch makes no network requests and works fully offline.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics that identify you.
  • Stitch only ever sees the screenshots you explicitly pick through Apple's photo picker. It cannot browse your photo library.
  • Saving uses add-only access: Stitch can add your combined image to Photos but can never read anything else.
  • Temporary files created for sharing are deleted automatically.
  • Your original screenshots are never modified.

Purchases are processed by Apple; Stitch never sees payment details.

If this policy ever changes, this page and the App Store listing will be updated first.
